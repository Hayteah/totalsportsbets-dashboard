## 2026-07-24 - Social Media Circular Cropping and ViewBox Safe Zones
**Learning:** Modern social networks (Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter) automatically crop uploaded profile pictures to circles. To keep vector logos fully visible, centered, and visually striking, the branding motif must lie entirely within a circular safe area (typically a radius of 40% of the total canvas width/height).
**Action:** Always verify SVG vertex positions mathematically or visually against a circular overlay when designing standard 1000x1000 profile picture assets.
