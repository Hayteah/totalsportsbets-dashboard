## 2026-07-30 - Branding Asset Generation & Platform Icon Integration
**Learning:** Standardized SVG vectors are highly consistent and scalable. Combining circular container styling with standard SVGs yields highly polished UI components that handle responsive scaling and circular crops naturally across both native browser previews and actual social networks.
