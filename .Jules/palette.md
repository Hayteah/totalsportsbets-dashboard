## 2026-08-03 - [Theme Branding Asset Generation]
**Learning:** Designing consistent SVG-based profile picture assets using a unified viewport (1000x1000) with a vertically/horizontally centered lightning bolt and background mask prevents crop distortion on various social media platforms (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Twitter).
