## 2026-08-09 - Crop-Safe Social Media Branding Assets
**Learning:** When embedding SVGs as social profile pictures, centering the branding icon vertically and horizontally inside a square viewBox containing a background rectangle ensures crop-safety when social platforms crop PFPs into a circle.
**Action:** Always design profile picture SVG assets with a 1000x1000 viewBox containing a centered logo element and full-bleed background rect to prevent circular clipping from cropping out important logo details on platforms like Instagram and Twitter.
